\subsection{Delayed Repair Adapter}
\label{sec:repair}
We present the Delayed Repair Adapter, which targets the hidden states during answer generation to mitigate the impact of information loss. We acknowledge that the negative effects of pruning are most prominent during the final response synthesis, so we encode the discarded features into a compact context vector at each pruning stage. Then, we utilize this cached information in a later repair layer to modulate the feature distribution.

\begin{equation}
    e_{\text{mask}} = \text{proj}\left( \text{softmax}(q \cdot (m \odot P)^\top) \cdot (m \odot P) \right)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    e_{\text{pruned}} = \text{proj}\left( \frac{\sum_i (1-m_i) h_i}{\sum_i (1-m_i)} \right)
\end{equation}

Where $e_{\text{mask}}$ represents the spatial embedding of the pruning mask and $q$ is a learnable attention query while $P$ denotes the position encodings. The term $e_{\text{pruned}}$ signifies the aggregated hidden states of the discarded tokens, and $h_i$ represents the hidden state of the $i$-th visual token. A Feature-wise Linear Modulation mechanism is employed within the repair module, which adjusts the attention outputs based on the cached context.

\begin{equation}
    \text{cond} = e_{\text{mask}} + e_{\text{pruned}} + \text{QueryProj}(q_{in})
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \gamma = 1 + W_\gamma \cdot \text{cond}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \beta = W_\beta \cdot \text{cond}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \Delta h = \alpha \cdot \text{Up}(\gamma \odot \text{GELU}(\text{Down}(x)) + \beta)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    h_{out} = x + \mathbf{g} \odot \Delta h
\end{equation}

Where $\text{cond}$ represents the fused context vector and $q_{in}$ is the query of the current token while $\gamma$ and $\beta$ are the scaling and shifting parameters of the FiLM module. The variable $x$ denotes the input hidden state and $\alpha$ is a learnable scaling factor. The term $\Delta h$ represents the repair increment and $\mathbf{g}$ is a binary mask that restricts the repair to the answer generation region. By employing this delayed compensation, we restore the feature distribution specifically where it is most critical for answer generation.

\subsection{Delayed Repair Adapter}
\label{sec:repair_clarified}
We present the Delayed Repair Adapter, which mitigates pruning-induced representation drift during answer generation. The representation gap introduced by token pruning can be subtle in early layers but becomes increasingly exposed as decoding proceeds to deeper layers. We therefore cache compact pruning-related context at each pruning stage and use it later at designated repair layers to modulate hidden states.

\begin{equation}
    e_{\text{mask}} = \text{proj}\left( \text{softmax}(q \cdot (m \odot P)^\top) \cdot (m \odot P) \right)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    e_{\text{pruned}} = \text{proj}\left( \frac{\sum_i (1-m_i) h_i}{\sum_i (1-m_i)} \right)
\end{equation}

Let $m\in[0,1]^N$ denote the keep mask over $N$ visual tokens produced by the pruner, where larger values indicate that a token is retained. We construct a mask embedding $e_{\text{mask}}$ by attending over masked positional encodings and projecting the pooled result. We also summarize the discarded tokens by a masked average of their hidden states, producing $e_{\text{pruned}}$, where $h_i$ denotes the hidden state of the $i$-th visual token.

\begin{equation}
    \text{cond} = e_{\text{mask}} + e_{\text{pruned}} + \text{QueryProj}(q_{in})
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \gamma = 1 + W_\gamma \cdot \text{cond}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \beta = W_\beta \cdot \text{cond}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    \Delta h = \alpha \cdot \text{Up}(\gamma \odot \text{GELU}(\text{Down}(x)) + \beta)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    h_{out} = x + \mathbf{g} \odot \Delta h
\end{equation}

We fuse the cached context with the current token query $q_{in}$ to form $\text{cond}$. The repair module uses feature-wise linear modulation to generate per-dimension scale $\gamma$ and shift $\beta$ from $\text{cond}$, and modulates a bottleneck transformation to obtain a residual correction $\Delta h$. The additive form $1+W_\gamma\text{cond}$ encourages an identity initialization and the learnable scalar $\alpha$ controls the correction magnitude. Finally, we apply the correction only to the answer-generation region using a binary mask $\mathbf{g}$, since representations in this region directly determine the final output distribution.
